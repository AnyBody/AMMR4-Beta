# Rat Hindlimb

:::{anylink-gallery}
:margin:
:::

The Ulm rat hindlimb model is a simulation of the internal forces and moments in
the femur of a rat during gait. The model is based on the work of Tim Wehner et al. [^cite_tim] from Ulm University, Germany. The model is based on micro CT scans
of the lower extremity of 15 rats. Kinematics was created from x-ray movies in
two different planes. This model is a good example of small, stand alone models developed to answer specific research questions.

Please cite the aforementioned paper when using or publishing with this model.
